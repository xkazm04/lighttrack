Health: /health is the operator rollup (`ok`, or 503 naming the red member). The kubelet reads
/health/live for "is the process wedged" (a red restarts the pod) and /health/ready for "should
traffic arrive" (a red removes it from the Service). A NotReady pod is QUIET — nothing here pages
you — so if the store is unreachable you will see it as an empty Service, not as an alert:
  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name=lighttrack
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "lighttrack.fullname" . }} 8787:{{ .Values.service.port }} && curl http://127.0.0.1:8787/health

The lt-runner judge/queue worker runs OUTSIDE the cluster (it needs the `claude` CLI + provider keys):
  lt-runner --base <service-url> --key <admin-or-project-key> serve --interval 10
